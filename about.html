made slowly

cristina's closet is a one-woman studio in new hampshire. every piece starts as a design i draw myself, in fabric i find locally, and gets cut and sewn by hand in my home.

i make very few of anything. not as a trick — that's just how long it takes to do it properly. when an edition is gone, i retire it and start the next one.

i'd rather you own one thing that lasts twenty years than twenty things that last one. quality should stand the test of time, so that's the only standard here.

add a line or two in your own words: how you started sewing, or what your town is like.

— cristina
